Case study04 / 05

Funded in dollars, booked in pounds.

The office is funded in USD and spends in EGP, while project costs are tracked in SAR. I set one rule for rates so that the books, the bank and the cost reports agree.

  • Receipts booked at the actual conversion rate on the day received
  • USD balance kept in its own account, revalued at month end
  • Rate differences go to one FX account, never buried in expenses
  • EGP and SAR figures are never added together in a report

Method

How I close a month.

1DAY

Accruals. Bills paid this month for last month go back to last month. Depreciation and prepaid insurance spread monthly.

2DAY

Custody. Every advance settled with receipts, each expense posted in the month it was spent. Petty cash counted.

3DAY

Banks. Every account reconciled. USD balance revalued. Suspense account cleared to zero.

4DAY

Review. Trial balance, payroll tie-out, budget against actual with notes on each variance.

5DAY

Report and lock. Management report sent, read-only copy to the group, period locked.

How I work

Four rules I keep.

I

Every number has a document. Each entry links to its receipt, statement or signed sheet.

II

Every expense in its month. Paid late or early, it lands in the month it belongs to.

III

Nothing leaves unsigned. Payroll and overtime go to management only after approval.

IV

Bad news early. A funding gap reported two weeks ahead is a plan. Reported on payday, it's a crisis.
